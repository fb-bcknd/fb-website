What are some interesting historical facts about The Five Bells?

The Five Bells is more than just a local pub. It’s a historic, Grade II listed Victorian public house that has been part of New Cross for generations.

The pub also has a fascinating literary connection. Charles Dickens wrote Our Mutual Friend at The Five Bells while lodging nearby.

It’s equally well known for its connection to Millwall Football Club supporters, having served as a popular pre-match gathering place for generations. Photographs from 1988, preserved by the London Picture Archive, even capture Millwall fans gathering at the pub.

From Victorian literary history to football traditions, The Five Bells has a rich heritage and remains an important part of the New Cross community.
